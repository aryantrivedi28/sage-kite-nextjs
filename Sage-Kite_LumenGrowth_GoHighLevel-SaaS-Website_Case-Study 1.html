The challenge

A GoHighLevel SaaS launch needed a website that could sell in five currencies and send each buyer to the right checkout

LumenGrowth was launching a white-label SaaS product on GoHighLevel. Native GoHighLevel cannot display currency switching, so a standard page could not show a buyer in Dublin, Toronto or Sydney their own currency. The website had to close that gap, and also explain a new product clearly enough to earn a free trial.

Selling in more than one currency

Prices had to appear in GBP, USD, EUR, CAD and AUD, which native GoHighLevel pages cannot switch between.

Joining the site to billing

Every plan button had to open the matching GoHighLevel SaaS checkout, in the right currency and billing period.

Earning trust for a new product

A new SaaS brand needs clear terms, a guarantee, plain-English pricing and a way to try the product before buying.

Custom pricing page

A multi-currency pricing page for GoHighLevel SaaS, with a currency selector and monthly or yearly billing

Native GoHighLevel cannot display currency switching, so we built the pricing page ourselves. Visitors pick a currency (GBP, USD, EUR, CAD or AUD) and a billing period, see Starter, Pro and Elite, and move straight to checkout. The page also carries a plain-English comparison table and a pricing FAQ.

Illustration of how the live pricing page behaves. On the real page the prices in each currency are shown beside each plan and every button opens the matching checkout.

  • Plain-English plan comparisonA "Compare plans" table sets out what Starter, Pro and Elite include, down to user numbers.
  • The setup fee explained up frontA section above the plans explains why there is a one-time setup fee and what it covers, so the price is not a surprise.
  • Ten pricing questions answered on the pageThe FAQ covers the free trial, supported currencies, setup fees, cancellation and what happens after payment.

Checkout wiring

Plan buttons wired to GoHighLevel SaaS checkout, so every visitor pays in the right currency

Each call to action on the pricing page opens the native GoHighLevel SaaS checkout for that plan, currency and billing period. Checkout runs on Stripe, so the website, the billing and the customer's new account all work as one flow.

  1. Landing pagelumengrowth.ai
  3. Pricing pageCurrency and plan chosen
  5. CheckoutGHL SaaS checkout, Stripe billing
  7. AccountCustomer account set up
  9. Loginapp.lumengrowth.ai

The journey follows the live site: plan buttons on the pricing page open checkout pages on the plans subdomain, and the Login link goes to the app subdomain.

Landing page

A SaaS landing page for an AI receptionist, built to turn visitors into free trials and demo bookings

The homepage leads with one clear promise, "Never Miss Another Service Call, Even While You Sleep", then explains the offer in plain words and gives the visitor two next steps: start a free trial, or book a demo.

A clear promise and one purpose

A single H1 states what the product does for a UK trades business, with a go-live-in-48-hours line underneath.

A live number to call and try

A "try our AI receptionist now" panel shows a phone number with a copy button, so a visitor can hear the product work before signing up.

A booking section for demos

A "Book your demo" section lets a visitor choose a time to see the product, for buyers who want a walkthrough first.

Risk-reducers beside the buttons

The 14-day trial, 30-day money-back guarantee and done-for-you setup sit in the hero, where the decision is made.

A chat widget for quick questions

A "Have a question?" chat sits on the page, so a hesitant visitor can ask before deciding.

Responsive and fast to scan

The pages are built mobile-friendly, so a tradesperson on a phone can read, call and sign up without pinching or zooming.

Domain and site structure

Domain connection and site structure: one brand across the website, checkout, app and legal pages

The marketing site lives on the main domain, and each supporting job has its own branded subdomain. A buyer never leaves the LumenGrowth brand, from first visit to first login.

Content, SEO and trust

SaaS website SEO and trust: a keyword-led blog, structured data, FAQs and compliant legal pages

A new product needs to be found, understood and believed. The site supports all three.

  • Titles that say who it is forPage titles name the product and the audience: UK trades and service businesses.
  • Structured data for the productSoftware product markup describes LumenGrowth as a business application with a GBP price range, and each page declares its canonical address.
  • A blog aimed at real buyer questionsSix articles cover missed calls, AI receptionists versus voicemail, setup in 48 hours, the included website, tool consolidation and the guarantee.
  • Legal pages that support SMSPrivacy policy, terms and a refund policy are live, with STOP and HELP language in the terms for SMS compliance.
