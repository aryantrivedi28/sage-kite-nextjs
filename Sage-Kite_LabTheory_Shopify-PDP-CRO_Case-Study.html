The challenge

A men's skincare brand needed its Shopify product pages to convert better and its COD returns reduced

For LabTheory, the product page is where a skincare shopper decides. The brand wanted the PDP to convert more visitors, to inform as well as attract, and to carry content that search engines can understand. Cash on delivery orders also needed attention, because return to origin (RTO) is a common cost of cash on delivery.

A product page that converts more

The PDP needed a new UI, built with CRO strategies, to turn more visitors into orders.

More information, easier to find

Shoppers needed more product information, and search engines needed SEO-friendly content to read.

RTO on cash on delivery

Cash on delivery orders can come back as RTO, so payment methods were targeted to reduce it.

Payments

Reducing RTO on cash on delivery orders with targeted payment methods on Shopify

RTO, or return to origin, is when a cash on delivery parcel is not accepted and travels back to the seller. We implemented targeted payment methods to reduce RTO on COD orders.

Where targeted payment methods sit in the order flow

Results

Over 5x more orders after the Shopify product page redesign: results from Shopify Analytics

With the new UI, orders jumped by over 5x. The store's own Shopify Analytics for the last 90 days shows orders up 422%, which is about 5.2 times the comparison period, and gross sales up 445%.
