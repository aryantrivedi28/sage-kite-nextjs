The problem

A premium jewellery brand stuck on a legacy WordPress and WooCommerce store

Esma required a robust digital transformation. The brand needed to move away from its legacy WordPress and WooCommerce environment to a scalable, premium Shopify storefront, one that preserved its historical data while adding features to drive conversion and provide deeper business intelligence.

A scalable, premium storefront

A Shopify store with the look and feel of a high-end brand, ready to grow, in place of a legacy WooCommerce setup.

Historical data preserved

Customer data, order history and product catalogs had to come across intact, with nothing lost in the move.

Features that convert and report

Advanced functionality to drive conversion rates and give the business deeper business intelligence.

The solution

A full-stack Shopify migration and development plan, built around data integrity and user experience

We executed a full-stack migration and development strategy in four parts. Jump to any of them below.

  • Platform migrationWordPress and WooCommerce to Shopify, with backups
  • Premium storefrontBuilt from scratch on the official Prestige theme
  • Conversion optimizationCustom checkout button logic
  • Business intelligenceCustom analytics reports for the client's KPIs

Platform migration

WooCommerce to Shopify migration without losing customers, orders or products

The entire ecosystem was migrated from WordPress and WooCommerce to Shopify. That included a secure transfer of customer data, order history and product catalogs, with comprehensive data backups to ensure zero loss.

How the data moved

Backed up, transferred and live on Shopify. Zero downtime and no data lost during the switch.

Illustrative diagram of the migration, not a record of individual data.

Premium storefront design

A premium Shopify storefront built on the official Prestige theme

The store was developed from scratch using the official Shopify Prestige theme, chosen for its high-end aesthetic and its suitability for D2C storytelling. The result is a premium, responsive interface built on the Prestige architecture.

Screenshots of the live storefront. Visit esmastore.com

Conversion rate optimization

Conversion rate optimization: custom checkout button logic to improve the add-to-cart rate

Custom checkout button logic was engineered for the store. This bespoke functionality was designed specifically to streamline the path to purchase, directly improving the add-to-cart (ATC) rate.

The custom logic sits where interest turns into purchase, shortening the path from add to cart to checkout.

Illustrative path to purchase, not a measured funnel. No conversion figures were provided for this project.

Custom Shopify analytics reports built around the client's KPIs
