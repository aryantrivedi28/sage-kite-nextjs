Who is Jobber for?

Jobber is built for home-service and field-service businesses whose work happens at a customer's property — where quoting, scheduling crews, tracking jobs and getting paid efficiently are the core operation.

Lawn care & landscaping

Recurring visits, routing efficiency, quoting for seasonal cleanups and project work.

Cleaning services

Scheduling crews, managing client preferences, job checklists and recurring residential or commercial cleans.

HVAC, plumbing & electrical

Dispatching technicians, quoting with options, on-site invoicing and capturing signatures.

Roofing, painting & exterior

Estimating projects, scheduling multi-day jobs, managing deposits and milestone payments.

Pest control & recurring services

Automated scheduling for seasonal treatments, chemical tracking notes and contract renewals.

Multi-crew operations

Any business needing a central schedule to dispatch multiple teams and track what happens in the field.

Note: If your work is office-based or primarily B2B professional services, Jobber is likely the wrong tool. We implement HubSpot and Dubsado for those operations instead.

The problem

01. Quoting

Inconsistent pricing

Different estimators use different spreadsheets. Quotes take too long to build and send, and follow-ups only happen when someone remembers.

02. Scheduling

Messy dispatch

Whiteboards and text messages mean crews drive inefficient routes, show up without the right equipment, or miss appointments entirely.

03. Jobs

Missing details

The field team does not have the client notes they need, and the office has to chase them for photos, signatures and job completion status.

04. Payments

Chasing invoices

Invoices are created manually days after the job is finished. Cash flow suffers because collecting payment requires a phone call or a bank transfer.

05. Recurring work

Dropped visits

Seasonal or monthly maintenance is tracked in a spreadsheet. Visits get missed, and recurring revenue slips through the cracks.

06. Growth

Can't add crews

The business relies on the owner holding the schedule in their head. You cannot hire another team because the current system would break.

Jobber solves these when configured properly.

Frequently asked questions

Jobber CRM services

What are Jobber CRM services?

Jobber is field-service management software for home-service businesses, with client management built in. Jobber CRM services cover configuring that platform around how your business runs: client records, tags and job history, requests and online booking, quoting, scheduling and dispatch, jobs and job forms, client communication, invoicing and payments, recurring work and automations. Sage Kite maps how you actually operate first, then sets Jobber up to match, tests it and hands it over.

What does a Jobber specialist do?

A Jobber specialist designs how the platform should reflect your operation, then configures it and sets up the automations that reduce office admin. That means quote templates that match how you price, a schedule and dispatch flow that fits your crews, recurring work set up properly, automated reminders and follow-ups, and invoicing and payments that run themselves — plus training so the office and field teams use it consistently.

Isn't Jobber easy enough to set up myself?

For a solo operator, often yes — Jobber is built to work quickly, and it includes onboarding and data import. The value of a specialist shows up as you grow: when quoting needs to be consistent across a team, when several crews need dispatching, when recurring work and automations have to be reliable, or when an account set up in a hurry has become messy. We are honest about this in discovery rather than selling a setup you do not need.

Scope and configuration

Can Sage Kite set up a new Jobber account?

Yes. A new setup starts by mapping your operation from request to payment and repeat work, then configuring client records, request forms, quote and invoice templates, scheduling and dispatch, job forms, client communication and automations to match — and testing the flow before your team relies on it.

Can you audit and improve an existing Jobber account?

Yes. Many businesses start on Jobber themselves and outgrow the original setup: inconsistent quoting, scheduling that does not fit the crews, recurring work half-configured, automations that were never finished. We audit what is there, clean it up, rebuild the parts holding you back, and document it so the team can keep it running.

Can you set up quoting, scheduling and dispatch?

Yes. That covers quote templates with optional line items, approval through the client hub and automated follow-up on unapproved quotes; and a scheduling and dispatch flow — drag-and-drop calendar, crew assignment and routing — that matches how your teams actually work in the field.

Can you configure jobs, job forms and field workflows?

Yes. Jobs can carry the details, checklists and job forms your crews need on site, captured through the mobile app along with photos, notes, times and signatures. We set these up so the field team records the right information and the office sees it without chasing.

Can you set up recurring work and automations?

Yes. Recurring jobs and visits are one of Jobber's strengths for services like lawn care, cleaning and pest control, and we set them up so the schedule and invoices generate on their own. Automations handle appointment reminders, on-my-way texts, follow-ups and review requests around them.

Can you set up invoicing and payments?

Yes. Invoice templates generated from completed jobs, payment collection through Jobber Payments and the client hub, automated payment reminders, and syncing to accounting through the QuickBooks or Xero integrations where you use them.

Can you connect Jobber to the tools I already use?

Where they are supported. Jobber connects to tools like QuickBooks and Xero and others through its app marketplace and open API. We confirm what your stack allows in discovery so the systems stay in sync rather than needing double entry.
